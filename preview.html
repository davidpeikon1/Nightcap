Questions? We've Got Answers.

What exactly is Nightcap?

Nightcap is a functional evening beverage with zero sugar, designed to replace the drinks that are quietly wrecking your sleep and health. Think of it as the opposite of an energy drink — made with magnesium, L-theanine, chamomile, and adaptogens to help you wind down naturally.

When does it launch?

We're targeting Summer 2026. Waitlist members get first access, and the higher you are on the list, the earlier you'll be able to order. Take the quiz and refer friends to move up.

How is this different from sleepy-time tea?

We use clinically-dosed, bioavailable forms of each ingredient (like magnesium glycinate, not oxide). Most teas use trace amounts of herbs that don't actually move the needle. Nightcap is formulated to produce real, measurable results — and it tastes like something you'd order at a cocktail bar, not a health food store.

What does it taste like?

We've spent over a year on flavor. Expect something refreshing, lightly botanical, with a hint of citrus — naturally sweetened with monk fruit and allulose. Beta testers consistently say it's the best-tasting functional beverage they've ever had.

Is there caffeine?

Zero caffeine. Nightcap is designed for evening consumption. L-theanine (one of our key ingredients) actually promotes relaxation without causing drowsiness — it's the calming compound found in green tea, isolated without the caffeine.
