Questions? We've Got Answers.

I've tried quitting sugar before and failed. Why would this be different?

Because willpower alone doesn't work — and that's not your fault. Processed sugar triggers the same brain pathways as addictive substances, and it's hidden in 73% of packaged foods. Nightcap gives you the awareness to see it (quiz), the daily tools to track it (app), and eventually products that replace the craving at its source. You don't have to white-knuckle it anymore.

I don't eat that much sugar — do I really need this?

That's what everyone says before they take the quiz. Most people who think they eat "pretty healthy" discover they're consuming 2-4x the recommended daily amount. Sugar goes by 60+ names on labels — dextrose, maltose, HFCS, "evaporated cane juice." It's in your bread, your sauces, your yogurt. Take the 60-second quiz and see your real number.

What is Nightcap exactly?

Nightcap is building a complete system to help you leave processed sugar behind. It starts with awareness (this quiz), continues with daily tracking and habit-building (the app), and will eventually include products designed to replace sugar cravings at their source. We're not a diet. We're not a fad. We're building the tools that actually make quitting stick.

Will I have to give up everything I enjoy?

No. This isn't about deprivation — it's about replacing processed sugar with things that make you feel better, not worse. Most people find that once they see where sugar is hiding and start making targeted swaps, they don't miss it. The cravings fade. The energy improves. And you get to enjoy food without the crash that comes after.

What happens after I take the quiz?

You'll see your personalized sugar report — your daily, weekly, and yearly intake, broken down by source. You'll get a specific action plan based on your answers. And you'll get early access to the Nightcap app and community of people who are doing this together. You're not in this alone.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your quiz answers and personal info are never sold, shared, or used for advertising. We use your data only to give you a personalized sugar report and to build better tools that help you reduce. Unsubscribe from any communication at any time.
