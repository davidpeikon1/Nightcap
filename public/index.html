The Science

A sugar problem
hiding in plain sight.

Processed sugar is in 74% of packaged foods sold in the U.S. It's been linked to obesity, type 2 diabetes, heart disease, and cognitive decline — yet most people can't name more than a handful of sources in their own diet.

Methodology

How we calculate
your number.

Five questions, five data points, one estimate calibrated against USDA nutritional databases and peer-reviewed dietary intake studies. No guesswork — just honest math.

  1. 01

    Breakfast profile

    We map your morning choices against average sugar content from USDA FoodData Central — 375,000+ foods indexed by serving.

  2. 02

    Daily beverages

    Sweetened drinks are the single largest source of added sugar in the American diet. We quantify your liquid intake precisely.

  3. 03

    Packaged foods

    Seventy-four percent of packaged foods contain added sugar. We estimate your exposure based on how much of your diet is processed.

  4. 04

    Label awareness

    Whether you read labels is a strong predictor of hidden sugar exposure. We factor this into the final estimate.

  5. 05

    Context calibration

    Your motivation shapes how we present your report — the number is the same, the framing is yours.
