🔎 "Healthy" Breakfast Foods

A bowl of granola with vanilla yogurt and a glass of orange juice? That's 45-60g of sugar before you leave the house. The same as eating 4 Krispy Kreme donuts. Most "healthy" breakfast options are sugar bombs in disguise.

🔎 Sauces, Dressings & Condiments

2 tbsp of ketchup = 8g of sugar. Salad dressing = 5-12g per serving. BBQ sauce = 16g. These "small" additions throughout the day can add 20-40g of hidden sugar without you ever tasting sweetness.

🔎 Bread & "Savory" Foods

Two slices of white bread = 6g of sugar. Pasta sauce = 10g per serving. Frozen pizza = 12g. Processed sugar isn't just in sweet foods — it's in nearly everything that comes in a package.

🔎 The 60+ Names for Sugar

Dextrose. Maltose. Sucrose. High fructose corn syrup. Evaporated cane juice. Agave nectar. Brown rice syrup. If you don't know what to look for, you can't avoid it. The Nightcap app flags every one of these.
